# Veya Digital Marketing Analytics Case Study

## From Digital Reach to Customer Conversion

**Project Type:** Marketing Analytics Case Study  
**Company:** Veya  
**Industry:** Beauty & Skincare E-commerce  
**Market:** Pakistan  
**Analyst Role:** Junior Marketing Analyst

---

## Case Study Overview

This case study follows the marketing analytics journey of Veya, a fictional Pakistani beauty and skincare e-commerce brand.

The case study explores how digital marketing data can be used to understand campaign performance, customer conversion, marketing efficiency, and return on investment.

The analysis follows the stages:

**Ask → Prepare → Process → Analyze → Share → Act → Wrap-Up**

> **Note:** Veya is a fictional company, and the dataset used in this case study will be simulated for educational and portfolio purposes.

---

# 1. Introduction

## Background

Digital marketing has become an important part of how e-commerce businesses attract customers, generate sales, and build long-term relationships with their audiences.

For a growing e-commerce brand, simply spending money on advertising is not enough. Marketing teams need to understand which channels and campaigns are generating meaningful results and where improvements can be made.

Veya is facing this challenge as it expands its digital presence in the Pakistani beauty and skincare market.

The company currently uses several digital marketing channels, including:

- Instagram
- Facebook
- Google Ads
- TikTok
- Email Marketing

Although these channels generate traffic and customer interactions, Veya's marketing team wants to understand which campaigns are actually contributing to customer conversions and revenue.

The company therefore decides to use marketing analytics to evaluate its digital marketing performance.

## Purpose of the Analysis

The purpose of this case study is to analyze Veya's marketing data and transform it into actionable business insights.

The analysis will focus on:

1. Campaign performance
2. Customer acquisition
3. Conversion performance
4. Marketing spending
5. Revenue generation
6. Return on Advertising Spend (ROAS)
7. Channel performance

The findings will be used to develop recommendations that can help Veya improve its marketing strategy and allocate its advertising budget more effectively.

## Business Problem

Veya's marketing team is currently investing in multiple digital channels, but it does not have a clear data-driven view of which channels and campaigns provide the strongest business results.

The marketing team wants to answer an important question:

> **How can Veya use its marketing data to improve customer conversion and maximize the return from its digital marketing investment?**

This question will guide the entire analysis.

---

# 2. Scenario

## Business Situation

Veya is a growing Pakistani beauty and skincare e-commerce brand that is expanding its digital presence and investing more heavily in online marketing.

The company currently promotes its products through several digital marketing channels, including Instagram, Facebook, Google Ads, TikTok, and Email Marketing. These channels are used to increase brand awareness, attract potential customers, encourage website visits, and generate online sales.

As Veya's marketing investment increases, the marketing team wants to understand whether its advertising budget is being used effectively. Although the company collects campaign performance data, the data has not yet been analyzed in a structured way to support marketing decisions.

The Marketing Manager wants to answer several important questions:

- Which digital marketing channels perform best?
- Which campaigns generate the most conversions?
- Which campaigns generate the highest revenue?
- How efficiently is the advertising budget being used?
- Which channels provide the strongest return on advertising spend?
- Which campaigns may require optimization?
- Where are potential opportunities to improve customer conversion?

To answer these questions, the Marketing Manager has asked me to conduct a comprehensive marketing analytics investigation.

## My Role

I am working as a **Junior Marketing Analyst** responsible for analyzing Veya's digital marketing campaign data and translating the results into actionable marketing insights.

My responsibilities include:

1. Examining and understanding the available marketing data.
2. Cleaning and preparing the data for analysis.
3. Calculating relevant marketing performance metrics.
4. Comparing the performance of different marketing channels.
5. Evaluating individual campaign performance.
6. Analyzing customer conversion and marketing funnel performance.
7. Identifying important trends, patterns, and opportunities.
8. Creating data visualizations and an interactive marketing dashboard.
9. Presenting key findings to the marketing team.
10. Developing data-driven recommendations to improve marketing performance and budget allocation.

## Business Challenge

Veya does not want to evaluate its marketing performance based only on impressions, clicks, or advertising expenditure.

The company wants to understand the complete customer journey:

**Marketing Reach → Engagement → Conversion → Revenue → Return on Investment**

The central challenge is therefore to determine how Veya can use marketing analytics to improve customer acquisition, increase conversions, optimize campaign performance, and make more effective decisions about its digital marketing budget.

## Expected Outcome

The analysis should provide Veya's marketing team with a clear understanding of:

- The strongest and weakest marketing channels
- The highest- and lowest-performing campaigns
- Customer conversion performance
- Marketing spending efficiency
- Revenue generated by campaigns
- Return on Advertising Spend (ROAS)
- Opportunities for campaign optimization
- Potential improvements in marketing budget allocation

The final findings will be presented through an interactive dashboard, visualizations, and a set of actionable marketing recommendations.
